We are the data controller responsible for your information. This policy applies only to the App. It does not cover websites or services of third parties that the App may link to.

Data we collect

We aim to collect as little data as possible. Depending on the features you use, the App may process the following:

Information you provide

  • Account data, such as your name and email address, if you create an account or sign in.
  • Content you enter, such as text, notes, photos or files you add in the App.
  • Support messages you send us by email, including your email address and the contents of your message.

Information collected automatically

  • Crash logs and diagnostics: crash stack traces, app version, device model, operating system version, and technical events leading up to a crash.
  • App usage data: screens viewed, features used, session length and similar interaction events, collected in aggregated or pseudonymous form.
  • Device and app identifiers: an app instance ID or Firebase installation ID used to group crash reports and analytics. We do not collect your IMEI, serial number or phone number.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city level) by our analytics provider. We do not store your IP address ourselves.

The App does not collect your contacts, SMS, call logs, browsing history, or financial or health information.

How we use data

We use the information described above only to:

  • provide the App's features and keep your data in sync across devices;
  • detect, diagnose and fix crashes and bugs;
  • understand which features are used so we can improve the App;
  • respond to your support requests;
  • protect the App and its users against fraud and abuse;
  • comply with legal obligations.

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on the following legal bases: performance of a contract (providing the App), our legitimate interests (stability, security and improvement), your consent (where requested in the App), and compliance with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Data retention

  • Account data and content are kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days after you delete your account or ask us to delete it.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.
  • Support emails are kept for up to 2 years after the conversation ends.

Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Security

All data sent between the App and our service providers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Access to stored data is limited to the developer and is protected by access controls and two-factor authentication. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your rights and deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

How to delete your data

You can delete your account from the App's settings, if the App has accounts, or send a request to support_HolodStudio@proton.me with the subject "Data deletion request", the App name and the email address linked to your account. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

You can also opt out of analytics by turning off usage sharing in the App's settings (where available) and reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the changes are significant, we will notify you in the App before they take effect.
